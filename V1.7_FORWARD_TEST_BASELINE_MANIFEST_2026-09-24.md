This is a **prospective signal-time dataset**, not an outcome dataset.

The baseline preserves what the scanner recorded at signal time. Blank/`UNRECORDED` outcome fields remain unrecorded.

No fill, exit, realized R, slippage, MAE/MFE, or Missed Opportunity R is inferred from the observed prices.

## 6. V1.7 handling rule

This manifest freezes the 24-Sep-2026 population as the **baseline observation set**.

Future daily captures should be appended only after:
1. schema validation;
2. duplicate `capture_id` / `signal_key` checks;
3. timestamp validation;
4. explicit separation of signal-time fields from genuine execution outcomes.

The baseline is not used to claim positive or negative expectancy. Expectancy remains unproven until genuine outcome evidence exists across an adequately sized, auditable sample.
